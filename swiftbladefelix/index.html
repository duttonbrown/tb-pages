Your Launch Checklist

Do these once, in order, and this site is fully yours — live on swiftbladehair.com, $0/month hosting, and ChatGPT doing all the code. After launch, any update takes five minutes.

⬇  Download the Website Files One zip, everything inside — this page, the styles, your logo and photos.
  1. 1

    Get the files & preview

    Hit the download button above, then unzip it — you get a swiftbladefelix folder. Double-click index.html inside it: that's the whole site, running from your own computer. Everything else in the folder (photos, style.css) has to stay next to it.

  2. 2

    Make it yours with ChatGPT

    Hours changed? New service? Want different words or photos? The editing guide below shows the exact copy-paste routine — no code knowledge needed.

  3. 3

    Grab your permanent Square booking link

    In your Square Dashboard: Appointments → Online Booking → copy your booking page link (it looks like squareup.com/appointments/…). The Book buttons on this site currently point at your old Square website's booking page, which disappears in step 6 — so swap in the permanent link first. There's a ready-made ChatGPT prompt below.

  4. 4

    Put the site online — free

    Go to netlify.com/drop and drag the whole swiftbladefelix folder onto the page. The site is live in about 30 seconds at a free web address. Make a free Netlify account so you can keep it and update it later.

  5. 5

    Connect swiftbladehair.com

    In Netlify: Domain settings → Add custom domain → type swiftbladehair.com and follow its DNS instructions wherever you pay for the domain (likely Square or GoDaddy). The switch takes 15 minutes to 24 hours. Your old site keeps working until then — no gap.

  6. 6

    Cancel ONLY the Square Online site plan

    Once swiftbladehair.com shows the new site, cancel the Square Online (website) subscription. Keep Square Appointments — that's your booking, payments, and client list. Nothing changes for your clients.

  7. 7

    Update your listings

    Point your Instagram and TikTok bio links at the site, and make sure Square and your Google Business Profile both show 711 W Lake St — some listings still show the old Franklin Ave address, which sends new clients to the wrong place.

Everyday Edits with ChatGPT

You don't need to know code. ChatGPT can make any change to this site for you — just tell it what you want. Here's exactly how.

01

Open your site file

Find the file index.html on your computer (in the swiftbladefelix folder). Right-click → Open With → Notepad (or TextEdit on Mac). Select all (Ctrl+A), copy (Ctrl+C).

02

Go to ChatGPT

Open chat.openai.com — free account works great. Start a new chat.

03

Use this prompt

I have a personal salon website as a single HTML file. I want to [describe your change here]. Please give me the complete updated index.html file with that change made. Keep everything else exactly the same. Here is the current file:

[paste your index.html here]

ChatGPT gives you the updated file. Copy it, paste back into Notepad over the old contents, save (Ctrl+S). Double-click index.html to check it in your browser.

04

Publish the update

Log into Netlify, open your site, go to Deploys, and drag the swiftbladefelix folder in again. The live site updates in seconds.

Ready-to-use prompts — just fill in the blanks

  • Swap in your Square booking link
    "Change every booking link from 'swiftbladehair.com/s/appointments' to my permanent Square booking link: [paste your squareup.com/appointments link]. Keep everything else the same."
  • Change hours
    "Update my business hours to: Thursday–Sunday [time]. Monday–Wednesday Closed. Update the hours everywhere they appear, including the open/closed badge logic in the script. Keep everything else the same."
  • Add a service
    "Add a new service called '[name]' to the services list. Give it the next roman numeral. Keep everything else the same."
  • Remove a service
    "Remove '[service name]' from the services list and renumber the remaining services. Keep everything else the same."
  • Add prices
    "Add prices next to each service, like this: Balayage $[amount], Fades $[amount], [continue for each]. Style them subtly so the design stays clean. Keep everything else the same."
  • Update address
    "Update my address to '[new address]' everywhere it appears, including the Google Maps links and the hidden SEO data near the top of the file. Keep everything else the same."
  • Change phone number
    "Update my phone number to [new number] everywhere it appears. Keep everything else the same."
  • Add a review quote
    "Add a customer review quote '[review text]' in the about section below the current one. Keep everything else the same."
  • Swap the photo
    "Replace the salon photo (felix-salon.jpg) with a new image file called '[your-photo.jpg]'. Keep everything else the same." (Put the new photo in the same folder as index.html first.)
  • Change the tagline
    "Change the tagline 'Sharp cuts. Real vibes. Minneapolis made.' to '[your new tagline]' — and update the scrolling ticker text to match. Keep everything else the same."
  • Add a new section
    "Add a new section after [Services / About / Find Us] called '[section name]' with this content: [describe the content]. Match the style of the other sections. Keep everything else the same."

Make ChatGPT work better for you

  • Always say "keep everything else exactly the same" — stops ChatGPT from redesigning things you didn't ask it to touch.
  • If it only gives you part of the file, say "Give me the complete file, not just the changed section."
  • If something breaks, say "That broke something. Please revert to the original and only make the one change I asked for."
  • Save a backup of index.html before every change (copy it and rename the copy to index-backup.html) so you can always go back.
  • For bigger redesigns, paste the live site URL and say "Here's what it looks like: [URL]. I want to change the color / font / layout of [X]..."
